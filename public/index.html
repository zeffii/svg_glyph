Keys

    

        V / P / OSelect / pen / circle (drag from the centre; Shift-drag for an ellipse)
        MSwitch between Glyphs and Logo mode
        UPlace the underlay image: drag to move, ⊕ basepoint, ○ rotate, □ scale; double-click sets the basepoint
        Drop / paste imageAdd it as the underlay of the current glyph or drawing
        NShow / hide points, handles and markers, to see just the shapes
        GShow / hide the baseline and descender guides
        EscFinish contour, clear selection
        CClose (or open) the current contour
        IInsert a point on the segment under the cursor
        JJoin: hover an open end and it moves onto the other end, closing the contour with no extra segment. With two open ends selected, they weld into one contour at their midpoint (Shift+J: at the first one)
        Drag end → endDrag an open end onto another (within 8 screen pixels): it highlights, and after half a second asks “Weld? Y / N”
        D, then 1–9Divide the hovered segment (or segments between selected points) by adding that many evenly spaced points
        Shift-drag lineMove that segment (both its points)
        Ctrl+Shift-dragMove horizontally or vertically only
        R / KFillet / chamfer selected corners: move to size, click to apply
        Drag ⊕ / arcFillet: drag its centre or its arc to change the radius
        Click a circleShows its centre ⊕ and radius □: drag ⊕ to move, □ to resize (ellipses get one □ per axis)
        L, then LHover a line, L marks it; hover another, L makes it parallel
        DelDelete selected points
        ArrowsNudge 1 (Shift: 10)
        [ ]Previous / next glyph
        Ctrl+ASelect all points
        Ctrl+C X VCopy / cut / paste contours (nothing selected: whole glyph)
        Space-dragPan (or middle mouse)
        WheelZoom around the mouse position (also Ctrl+wheel and trackpad pinch); Shift+wheel scrolls sideways, Alt+wheel up and down
        AHover a control point: swing it into line with the point’s other handle (or the straight line on the other side), keeping its length
        SSmooth: keep the selected points' handles in line, each with its own length
        EEqual: give the selected points' handles the same length, mirrored while you drag
        Alt-dragBreak a smooth handle
        Chain iconClick to lock / unlink collinear arms: on a selected point (its two arms), or between a segment’s facing arms. Hover a point’s chain for (S) Smooth / (E) Equidistant
        + / −Arm / disarm the hovered control point; armed points move along with the one you drag (− with nothing hovered disarms all)
        Dbl-clickPoint: sharp/smooth. Segment: add point
        B / right-clickSegment under cursor: line ↔ curve (no hover: segments between selected points)
